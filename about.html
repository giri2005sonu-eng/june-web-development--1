Our Story

BookHive was born in 2024 from a simple idea — every Indian reader deserves easy access to the best books from around the world, at fair prices, delivered to their doorstep.

Founded in Bengaluru by a small team of book enthusiasts, we've grown into India's go-to destination for fiction, self-help, biographies, business books, and more. We believe books have the power to change lives, spark new ideas, and connect people across cultures.

Our Mission

At BookHive, our mission is to make reading accessible and enjoyable for everyone. We strive to provide a seamless online shopping experience, a wide selection of books, and personalized recommendations to help you discover your next favorite read.
